About Me

Hello, and welcome. I am Nilan Jayasinghe, an official Sworn Translator for English, Russian and Sinhala, appointed by the Ministry of Justice and Prison Reforms of Sri Lanka. I took my oath as a Sworn Translator at the District Court of Kalutara.

Official name on certified documents: B.M.D.N.S. Jayasinghe  |  Registered No. 2019/BL/KL/001

I have worked as a court interpreter in Russia since 2017 and in Sri Lanka since 2019, and I have completed more than 5,000 document translations. Whether you need a single birth certificate translated or a full set of court pleadings, I treat your documents with care and make the process simple for you.

What I Do

  • Court interpreting: On-site interpreting in High Courts, District Courts and Magistrate Courts, as well as at conferences.
  • Certified document translation: Court pleadings, corporate contracts, visa documents, and birth and degree certificates.
  • Language pairs: Russian → English, Sinhala → Russian, English → Sinhala.

Who I Work With

I support courts, government agencies, law firms, embassies, corporate exporters, foreign students and individuals who need reliable language support in legal matters.

Accuracy You Can Trust

  1. Draft review: You receive a draft first and confirm that everything is correct.
  2. Second-reviewer check: A second professional reviewer checks every document to catch errors.
  3. Certification: Once you approve the draft, I issue the final translation, signed and sealed by me as a Sworn Translator.
  4. Corner seal security: I use a corner seal, an anti-tamper measure that physically binds the translation to your original or certified copy, so the documents cannot be separated or altered.

Recognised Where You Need It

As a translator appointed by the Ministry of Justice, my translations are accepted by the Ministry. Once certified by the Ministry of Justice, your documents can be authenticated by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Sri Lanka, which allows them to be presented to foreign embassies.

Turnaround Times

  • Standard document translation: about 3 working days
  • Urgent translation: within 24 hours
  • Court or conference interpreting: please book at least 1 week in advance

Confidentiality

Your documents are often personal, legal or commercial, so I follow strict data protection protocols for all sensitive material. I am happy to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) on request.
